Privacy Policy

Business Saarthi • Enterprise POS & ERP Platform

1. Information We Collect

When you register on Business Saarthi, we collect basic business information required to provision your workspace:

  • **Account Details:** Business Name, Owner/Representative Name, Registered Mobile Number, and Email Address.
  • **Operational Data:** Inventory items, billing ledgers, stock quantities, and expense records entered into your ERP dashboard.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide, maintain, and improve your ERP workspace. We use Google Firebase cloud infrastructure to sync your inventory and bills in real-time across your devices. **We never sell, rent, or trade your business data to third-party advertisers.**

3. Payment & Financial Security

Business Saarthi does not store your credit card numbers, debit card details, or bank account passwords. All online subscription payments and UPI transactions are processed through RBI-compliant, PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (such as Razorpay or Paytm Business).

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your workspace data as long as your subscription or trial is active. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated billing history from our cloud servers, you can request immediate data deletion by contacting our developer desk.
