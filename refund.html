Cancellation & Refund Policy

Business Saarthi • Subscription & Billing Terms

1. Free Trial & Grace Periods

Business Saarthi provides introductory trial access and temporary 7-day grace periods (via UTR proof submission) so merchants can thoroughly test the POS, inventory, and billing features before making any financial commitment. We encourage all shop owners to utilize this trial period to evaluate the software.

2. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time by contacting our support desk or opting out of renewal. Upon cancellation, your workspace will remain fully active until the end of your currently paid billing cycle (e.g., the end of your 1-year annual plan). Once the period expires, your workspace will safely transition to a locked state without losing your historical data.

3. Refund Policy (Strictly Non-Refundable)

Because Business Saarthi is a digital cloud software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform where server resources, database storage, and license keys are instantly provisioned upon activation:

  • **All subscription payments made via UPI, QR Scan, Net Banking, or Cards are final and strictly non-refundable.**
  • We do not offer prorated refunds if you decide to stop using the software midway through an active annual subscription.

4. Exceptional Circumstances & Failed Transactions

If a technical glitch or banking error results in a **double debit** (e.g., your account was charged twice for the same annual invoice), our support team will verify the bank receipt and initiate a 100% full refund of the duplicate amount within **5 to 7 business days** to the original source of payment.
