About Business Saarthi

Empowering Small Retail Businesses & Entrepreneurs

Who We Are

**Business Saarthi** is a dedicated software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform designed specifically to streamline daily operations for small and medium-sized retail businesses across India. Built with simplicity and speed in mind, our platform eliminates the friction of traditional pen-and-paper bookkeeping.

What We Provide

We provide an all-in-one Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Point of Sale (POS) cloud dashboard. Our core software features include:

• **High-Speed Billing POS:** Generate clean, instant digital or printable invoices in seconds.

• **Smart Inventory & Stock Ticker:** Real-time tracking of item quantities with automatic low-stock and out-of-stock ticker alerts.

• **Expense & Ledger Management:** Seamlessly track shop expenditures and monitor daily financial reports from any tablet, mobile, or desktop device.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring affordable, enterprise-grade digital tools to local Indian shopkeepers without burdening them with complex software setups or heavy transaction commissions. We believe every shop owner deserves a reliable digital *Saarthi* (companion) to help their business thrive.
